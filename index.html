Saty ATR Levels: how to read the odds

Each arrow: once price touches a level, how often it reaches the next one the same day. Checked on SPX, 2007-2026 (5,000 days).

How to trade it

  1. Break and hold a trigger (Calls > / Puts <). First target: 38.2%, 80%.
  2. 38.2% touched = Golden Gate open. Target 61.8%, 64%. Stop back behind the trigger.
  3. Scale out as odds fall: 61.8% → 78.6% → 1 ATR. Keep a runner only.
  4. Between PC and a trigger = no trade. Wait for a trigger to break.

Early gates almost always finish. After noon, most fail. SPX 5m, Mar-Nov 2026.

Don't trust the odds when

  • The gate failed: price opened a gate, then fell back past the trigger.
  • Range already ~100% of ATR: only 15% of days move a full ATR.
  • Overnight /ES: odds were measured on regular hours. Rough until 9:30.
  • The trend is against you: a red 10m ribbon beats an 80% arrow up.
